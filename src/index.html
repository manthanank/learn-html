1. Semantic Structural Elements

September 4, 2026 by Manthan Ankolekar

HTML5 replaces generic <div> soups with meaningful tags that inform browsers, search engines, and assistive screen readers about the purpose and hierarchy of content.

Core Landmark Elements

  • <header>: Introductory content or navigational aids.
  • <nav>: Major navigational links.
  • <main>: The central, dominant content of the document.
  • <article>: Independent, self-contained composition (blog post, news item).
  • <section>: Thematic grouping of content with a heading.
  • <aside>: Tangentially related sidebars, callouts, or glossaries.
  • <footer>: Metadata, copyright, and author contacts.
